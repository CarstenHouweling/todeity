The game

Tode just wanted his morning tea

Then the bees came. Todeity is a 3D roguelite bullet hell: every run you build a new frog out of skills, brews and charms, and fight your way down through five arenas to the bottom of the hive.

Bullet hell

Dodge, dash, graze

Bees fire fans, rings and spirals; arena events sweep the field with curtains of stingers, closing rings and starbursts, and the hive runs real tactics: encirclements, phalanxes, synchronized volleys.

  • Hop-dash straight through bullets and bees
  • Near misses graze: they sparkle and charge your ultimate
  • Shred the hive too fast and it adapts: tougher bees, in packs
Build-crafting

Skills that fuse, brews that talk

Every level is a pick of three skills. They cast themselves, so you can focus on moving. Train two partners up and they fuse into one rainbow skill; master a skill and it casts twice.

  • 24 skills, 13 fusions, up to six at once
  • 45 brews from five talking tea spirits: Green Tea, Ember Black, Frost Mint, Chamomile and Storm Oolong, with Duos, Legendaries and cursed brews
  • 14 charms that stack and chain into each other
Ascension

From garden frog to God of Tea

Grow strong enough and Tode transforms, with a cut-scene: a quake, stars pouring into him, a burst that shatters reality. In his garden, the world transforms with him, from blossoms to lanterns, crystals, floating islands and a marble heaven.

Ultimates

Steep, storm or summon

After the first boss you choose an ultimate. The Steeping Beam is an enormous beam you steer through the swarm, or hold on a boss and watch it melt. The Thunder Tea Storm calls lightning down on everything. Summoning: Great Tode brings a giant battle toad that stomps, tongue-lashes and body-slams.

  • Every boss leaves a Royal Tea Chest: a reel spins and lands on one, three or five rewards
Raid bosses

He doesn't stay down

Queen Buzzbelle, the Hornet Shogun, the Seraph Queen and Empress Buzzbelle fight in stages: shields with circling crystals to smash, rituals to interrupt, falling stars to soak, blade storms, enrage timers and beam clashes you win by mashing.

Then the ground gives way and you fall into the Underworld to face Bee-zebub, a winged devil in a fire aura. Beat him, and he rises again in his true form.

Five arenas

A long way down

A new field every five waves, each bigger than the last, with its own bees, hazards, side objective and sky. The graphics grow richer the deeper you go.

The Wardrobe

54 things to wear, all earned

Hats, face, body, back and aura: crowns, a ronin helm, devil horns, a grand moustache, a golden breastplate, draped capes, butterfly and infernal wings, a steam jetpack, auras of petals, embers and stardust.

  • Four are free; the rest unlock with achievements, levels and divine forms
  • You wear it everywhere: in runs, in co-op and in gardens
Together

Gardens, parties and co-op

Your garden is a meeting place. Decorate it with 27 trinkets, pick a song and day or night, then open it up: other frogs hop in, play, and set off on a co-op run together.

🐸

Co-op for 2–4

Public rooms, Quick play or an invite link. Every player plays their own class, and runs survive a server restart.

🏡

Gardens to visit

Hop on each other's heads, swim in the pond and skip stones, kick the Tea Ball, boop frogs and trinkets, use 17 emotes and form a party.

📅

Daily Brew

A new seeded run every day, the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard, a daily trial and a daily boon.

🏆

Leaderboards

Score, deepest run, fastest Bee-zebub, Daily Brew and co-op boards. Every run goes on by itself, one best entry per frog.

🔥

Steeping vows

Ten vows up to Steep 25 for more score and Golden Leaves. New to bullet hells? Gentle Brew gives you extra hearts and a second life.

🫖

Tode's Pantry

Golden Leaves buy 13 permanent upgrades. Pick one of 17 keepsakes, chase 39 achievements, claim a daily gift and a kettle that brews rewards.

☁️

Cloud saves

Your progress follows you with a save code. An account level with seven ranks, from Tadpole to Todeity, glows around your frog in gardens.

📱

Plays anywhere

Keyboard and mouse or twin-stick touch controls. Installable to your Home Screen, and the graphics adapt to your device.

How it was made

Built in days, made from code

Todeity started as the frog-and-bees game from the JetBrains Academy course “Build a Game with an AI Coding Agent” and grew from there with Claude Code as an AI pair. Nothing is a bought asset: the frogs, bees, bosses, arenas and wearables are built from code, and the music, voices and sound effects are synthesized in the browser. Even the trailers are rendered by a script that drives the game frame by frame.
