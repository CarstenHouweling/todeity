# Todeity: Rise of the Tea God: submission kit

Everything for the JetBrains Academy Build Your Game Challenge post (submissions open Oct 19, 10:00 CEST).
Rules recap: start public posts with **#JetBrainsGameChallenge**; no third-party trademarks, recognizable
characters, memes or commercial music (Todeity uses none: all music and sound are generated in code).

## Feature highlights (for the longer post or the video captions)

- **Roguelite core, Hades-inspired:** 27 brews in 5 Blends (Green Tea, Ember Black, Frost Mint, Chamomile,
  Storm Oolong) with rarities, 8 Duo brews and 5 Legendaries, each offered by a talking tea spirit.
- **Ascension:** five transformations with cut-scenes: golden flame aura, spiky crest, lightning, a halo and a
  reality-warping god form. The spit evolves from green to radiant gold.
- **The world transforms:** blossoms and lanterns, crystals and floating islands, and finally a marble garden
  floating above a sea of clouds.
- **Ultimates:** Steeping Beam (keep moving while you sweep it), Thunder Tea Storm, Summoning: Great Tode.
- **Roguelite bullet hell:** bees and bosses fire fans, rings and spirals; tea bombs cancel bullets; kills drop
  tea leaves: every level is a new skill or a skill upgrade. Wave modifiers, meteor showers, falling boulders
  and bee squads that surround you, march in a phalanx or fire together.
- **14 skills** that auto-cast (Ember Comet, Leaf Glaive, Firefly Swarm, Tea Tornado, Orbiting Teacups…), up to six
  at once, so the action never stops.
- **Side objectives** open the way to each bigger arena: smash the hives, ring the temple bells, seal the rifts.
- **Bosses:** one per arena with cinematic entrances and rage phases: Queen Buzzbelle, the Hornet Shogun, the
  Seraph Queen, the Empress at wave 20, then a fall into the
  Underworld for the true finale against Bee-zebub, with huge beam clashes you win by mashing.
- **Five arenas:** garden → Sage Mountain → Sky Citadel → Tournament of the Gods → the Underworld, each with its
  own bee species (ronin hornets, seraph bees, void wasps, demon hornets); the graphics get smoother and shinier
  with every arena (smooth shading, glossy materials, filmic tone mapping).
- **Weather & time of day:** tea rain, gales, thunderstorms with lightning strikes, from morning to night.
- **Juice:** slow-motion finishers, hit-stop, kill-streak callouts ("Brewtal!"), procedural orchestral music.
- **Co-op for 2–4 players** with room codes; the garden grows with the party.
- **Meta progression & community:** Tode's Pantry upgrades, global leaderboard and a seeded Daily Brew.
- **Plays anywhere:** keyboard + mouse, or touch with auto-aim on phones; adapts quality to the device.

## Launch-day checklist (Oct 19, 10:00 CEST)

- Deploy the latest build the evening before and open the live site once (cold start), on desktop and on a phone.
- Paste the link in a private Discord channel to check the preview card (Discord caches previews: if it shows an
  old one, add `?v=2` to the link in the post).
- Post early: submissions appear in the order they arrive, and voting starts right away.
- During the week: answer comments, share a co-op invite in the thread, and mention the Daily Brew leader each
  day. Every 👍 on the official post counts, so share the post link (not just the game link) with friends.
- Keep the App Service running until at least Oct 28 (winners announced) so the jury can still play.
